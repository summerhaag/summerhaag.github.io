abstract: "Apollonian circle packings (ACPs) are a fractal created by continual inversion through 4 mutually tangent circles or a quadruple. An integral ACP is when all the curvatures (1/radius) are integers. The study of integral ACPs has many connections to geometry, number theory, group theory, etc. and leads to very nice illustrations. But how much theory of integral ACPs still holds if we look at quadratic rings of integers as the curvatures? Some aspects still hold but we gain many more types of packings that are not covered by the integral case. We will go over reduction theory for quadruples, how that is equivalent to reduction of binary quadratic fields, and how using the parameter space of Apollonian circle packings can be used to discover more about these packings. "

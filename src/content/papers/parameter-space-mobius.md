---
title: "The parameter space of Apollonian circle packings through Möbius transformations"
authors: "Summer Haag"
abstract: >
  An Apollonian circle packing is a fractal created by starting with three
  pairwise tangent circles and iteratively filling in circles tangent to all
  three. Using positive semidefinite binary quadratic forms one can build the
  parameter space of Apollonian circle packings over P¹(C). We show that this
  method of parameter space is equivalent to inversion through a point and
  extend the group action on P¹(C) to cover all equivalent packings.
date: 2026-10-26
status: unpublished
